# Podcaster tools inside Spotify for Artists

## One-liner
Bring podcast analytics, audience and payout views into the same place artists already use.

## Why it matters
A growing number of creators publish both music and podcasts, and today that means two dashboards, two mental models and two support paths. Unifying is not just tidier — it is the only way the promotion and audience tools we are building for artists become available to podcasters without building them twice. It depends on the creator console consolidation landing first.

## What ships
Podcast analytics and audience views in the artists surface, payout visibility consistent with the music side, and a shared identity so one login covers both. Built on the TALK-004 console API rather than duplicating it.

## Who it's for
Creators publishing in both formats; eventually all podcasters.

## Target outcome
One dashboard for dual-format creators, and podcast access to artist promotion tooling without a parallel build.

## Open questions
- Does the podcaster experience live inside the artists product, or does the artists product become the shared creator product?
- Sequencing against TALK-004 is tight. What is the minimum console API we can build against?
