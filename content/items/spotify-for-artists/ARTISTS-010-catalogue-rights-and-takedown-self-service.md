# Catalogue rights and takedown self-service

## One-liner
Let a rights holder see, claim and act on their catalogue without going through a support queue.

## Why it matters
Rights disputes and impersonation currently route through a manual process that is slow enough to cause real harm — an artist finding their work on someone else's profile waits days. Self-service is the only path that scales with a catalogue growing this fast. It is in Later because it depends on the rights ledger rebuild, not because it is unimportant.

## What ships
Nothing until PLATFORM-010 provides an authoritative rights record to act against.

## Who it's for
Rights holders, and artists dealing with impersonation or misattribution.

## Target outcome
Cut time-to-resolution on rights claims from days to hours once the underlying ledger exists.

## Open questions
- Self-service takedown is an abuse surface. What is the verification bar, and who carries the liability for a wrongful takedown?
