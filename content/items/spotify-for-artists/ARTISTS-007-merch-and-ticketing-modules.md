# Merch and ticketing modules

## One-liner
Artist-controlled merch and ticket placements on artist pages, fulfilled by partners.

## Why it matters
Artists earn far more from merch and live than from streaming, and we are the place their audience already is. Surfacing that inventory costs us little and materially changes what the artist relationship is worth to them, which is leverage in every other conversation. We do not want to be a commerce operator, which is why partner fulfilment is the whole design.

## What ships
Partner-fulfilled merch and ticket modules on artist pages, artist-side control of what appears and where, attribution back to the artist dashboard, and no inventory, payment or fulfilment liability on our side.

## Who it's for
Artists with existing merch or touring operations.

## Target outcome
Meaningful partner-attributed revenue for participating artists, with zero fulfilment obligations landing on us.

## Open questions
- Do we take a cut? Taking one changes the relationship; not taking one makes the business case rest entirely on retention.
- Partner coverage is uneven by market. Do we launch unevenly or wait?
