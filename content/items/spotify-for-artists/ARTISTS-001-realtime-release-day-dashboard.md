# Realtime release-day dashboard

## One-liner
Streams, saves, playlist adds and listener geography updating within minutes on release day, not the next morning.

## Why it matters
Release day is the one day an artist team is fully mobilised and able to act, and we were handing them yesterday's numbers. A marketing decision that lands twelve hours late is a decision not made. Closing the latency gap turned the dashboard from a reporting tool into an operational one, which is the difference between something artists check and something they run their day from.

## What ships
A streaming aggregation path with a few minutes' latency on release-day metrics, geographic and playlist attribution breakdowns, a comparison against the artist's own prior releases rather than an anonymous benchmark, and alerting on unusual movement.

## Who it's for
Artist teams, labels and distributors working a release.

## Target outcome
Become the surface artist teams keep open on release day. Adoption among tracked releases exceeded the target and stuck.
