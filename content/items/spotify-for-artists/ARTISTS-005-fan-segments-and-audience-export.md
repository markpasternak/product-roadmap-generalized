# Fan segments and audience export

## One-liner
Named, reusable audience segments an artist team can target promotion at or export to their own channels.

## Why it matters
Artists have a mental model of their audience — superfans, lapsed listeners, a specific city before a tour — and our analytics only shows them aggregates. Segments let them act on that model inside our promotion tools, which makes promotion measurably more effective and increases the value of buying it. Export is where the privacy line sits and needs to be drawn carefully.

## What ships
Segment definitions over engagement, recency and geography; segments as targeting inputs to self-serve promotion; aggregate-only export with a minimum segment size; and no individual listener identity leaving the platform under any path.

## Who it's for
Artist teams running their own marketing alongside our promotion tools.

## Target outcome
Segment-targeted campaigns outperform untargeted ones by enough to justify the feature, without a single individual-level disclosure.

## Open questions
- Where exactly is the minimum segment size, and does it differ by market regulation?
- Does "export" mean a file, or only an audience handle usable inside our own tools? The second is safer and much less useful.
