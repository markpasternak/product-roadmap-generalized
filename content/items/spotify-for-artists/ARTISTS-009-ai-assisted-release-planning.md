# AI-assisted release planning

## One-liner
Suggested release timing, market focus and promotion budget based on an artist's own catalogue performance.

## Why it matters
Most independent artists make release decisions with no data and considerable superstition. We hold the data that would inform those decisions and currently present it as charts they have to interpret. Turning analysis into recommendation is a large step up in usefulness and a large step up in responsibility — a bad recommendation costs an artist a release cycle.

## What ships
Undefined. Discovery needs to establish whether our recommendations would beat the artist's own judgement often enough to be worth giving.

## Who it's for
Independent artists without label marketing support.

## Target outcome
An honest backtest against past releases before any of this reaches an artist.

## Open questions
- How do we present confidence so a low-confidence suggestion is not acted on as if it were high?
- If an artist follows our advice and the release underperforms, what do we owe them?
