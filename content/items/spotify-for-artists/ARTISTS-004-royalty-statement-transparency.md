# Royalty statement transparency

## One-liner
Show an artist how a payment was calculated, down to the market and rights-holder split.

## Why it matters
The single most damaging thing about royalties is not the amount, it is that artists cannot check the arithmetic — which means every disagreement becomes a question of trust rather than a question of data. Publishing the derivation converts an adversarial conversation into a reconcilable one. It is also the right thing to do before anyone requires it of us.

## What ships
Per-statement derivation showing streams by market, applicable rate, rights-holder split and deductions; a reconciliation view against the artist's own distributor statement; export in the formats accountants actually use; and a dispute path with a defined response window.

## Who it's for
Artists and their business teams; secondarily distributors reconciling on their behalf.

## Target outcome
Reduce royalty disputes that turn out to be misunderstandings, and cut the time to resolve genuine ones.

## Open questions
- Some deductions are governed by contracts we cannot show. How much can we expose without breaching them?
- Do we surface the rights-holder split when it is the one number an artist most wants and most often cannot legally see?
