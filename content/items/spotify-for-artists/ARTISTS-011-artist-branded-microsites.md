# Artist-branded microsites

## One-liner
A hosted, artist-branded landing page pulling live catalogue, tour and merch data.

## Why it matters
Requested mostly by artists who already have a website and want it to update itself. That is a real annoyance and a small one. Parked because the maintenance surface — custom domains, artist-supplied assets, moderation of artist-controlled pages — is disproportionate to the benefit, and nothing else on the roadmap depends on it.

## What ships
Nothing.

## Who it's for
Artists maintaining their own web presence.

## Target outcome
None while parked.

## Open questions
- Would a well-documented public API cover the actual need without us hosting anything?
