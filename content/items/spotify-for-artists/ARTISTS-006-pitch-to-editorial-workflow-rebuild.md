# Pitch-to-editorial workflow rebuild

## One-liner
A pitching flow where an artist can see what happened to a submission instead of guessing.

## Why it matters
Editorial pitching is the most consequential opaque process we run — it materially affects a release and gives back nothing but silence. That silence is read as favouritism, and the perception is corrosive whether or not it is accurate. The fix is mostly about feedback and expectation-setting rather than changing how editorial decides.

## What ships
A structured pitch form with the fields editors actually use, submission status through the review window, aggregate outcome data so an artist can see how pitches like theirs typically fare, and a deadline model that stops rewarding whoever submits earliest.

## Who it's for
Every artist team pitching a release, disproportionately the independent ones without a label relationship.

## Target outcome
Every pitch reaches a terminal status the artist can see, and perceived fairness improves in the artist survey.

## Open questions
- How much outcome detail can we give without it reading as a promise, or as a scoring rubric to game?
