# Direct-to-fan subscriptions

## One-liner
Let fans subscribe to an individual artist for early access, exclusives and direct contact.

## Why it matters
It is the most-requested artist monetisation feature and the one with the largest strategic implications, because it puts us in a direct relationship between artist and fan that our existing licensing agreements were not written to contemplate. That is exactly why it sits in intake rather than on the roadmap — the product question is tractable and the rights and label question is not, yet.

## What ships
Nothing. This needs a rights and commercial position before any product work is justified.

## Who it's for
Artists with a committed fanbase; fans who already pay artists elsewhere.

## Target outcome
Reach a defensible internal position on whether this is possible under current label agreements.

## Open questions
- Does an artist subscription conflict with existing label exclusivity terms, and in how many of our top markets?
- Is the payment relationship ours or the artist's, and what does each imply for liability and for the Premium relationship?
