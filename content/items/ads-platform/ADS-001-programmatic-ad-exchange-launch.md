# Programmatic ad exchange launch

## One-liner
Opened our audio and video inventory to programmatic demand through a first-party exchange.

## Why it matters
Direct sales cannot fill inventory that grows faster than a sales team can, and the unsold remainder was pure loss. An exchange lets demand find inventory continuously and prices it honestly, which raised the floor on everything and gave us a real read on what our audience is worth. It also made us legible to buyers whose entire workflow assumes programmatic access.

## What ships
A first-party exchange with real-time bidding across audio, video and display inventory, integrations with the major demand-side platforms, floor and deal controls per market, and a settlement path that reconciles against direct-sold inventory rather than competing with it.

## Who it's for
Programmatic buyers and the agencies operating on their behalf.

## Target outcome
Materially raise fill on previously unsold inventory without depressing direct-sold pricing. Both held through the first full year.
