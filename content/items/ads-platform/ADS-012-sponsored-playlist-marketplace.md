# Sponsored playlist marketplace

## One-liner
Let brands sponsor editorial and algorithmic playlists as a named placement.

## Why it matters
Brands ask for this constantly and it is straightforward inventory to create. It sits in intake because the listener cost is unclear and possibly large — a sponsored editorial playlist trades on trust we spent a decade building, and we do not know the exchange rate. Worth understanding before anyone sells it.

## What ships
Nothing yet. Needs a listener-trust read before it is worth scoping.

## Who it's for
Brand advertisers seeking association rather than reach.

## Target outcome
Quantify the listener trust cost well enough to decide.

## Open questions
- Does sponsorship of an editorial playlist compromise the editorial signal that makes it valuable in the first place?
