# Generative creative assistant

## One-liner
Generate compliant audio and video ad creative from a brief, for advertisers who have no creative team.

## Why it matters
Creative production is the real barrier for small advertisers — not budget, not targeting. They abandon at the upload step because they have nothing to upload. Generating a competent first draft removes the single largest drop-off in the self-serve funnel, and the quality bar for "better than nothing" is low. The risk is that we start producing ads we are accountable for.

## What ships
Brief-to-creative generation for standard audio and video formats, automatic format and policy validation before it can run, synthetic voice with clear labelling, and mandatory advertiser review and approval before anything serves.

## Who it's for
Self-serve advertisers without in-house creative.

## Target outcome
Cut the self-serve drop-off at the creative step substantially, with generated creative performing within a reasonable band of uploaded creative.

## Open questions
- Who is liable for a generated claim that turns out to be false — us, or the advertiser who approved it?
- Do we allow synthetic voices that resemble real people at all, or is the answer simply no?
