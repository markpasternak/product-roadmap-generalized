# Advertising in audiobooks

## One-liner
An ad-supported audiobook tier for listeners past their included allowance.

## Why it matters
Technically straightforward and commercially plausible. Parked because publisher agreements do not currently permit advertising against audiobook content, and reopening those terms would put the far more valuable subscription bundle back on the table. Not worth the risk for the revenue on offer.

## What ships
Nothing.

## Who it's for
Listeners unwilling to pay for a top-up.

## Target outcome
None while parked.

## Open questions
- Is there a publisher subset who would agree without reopening the master terms?
