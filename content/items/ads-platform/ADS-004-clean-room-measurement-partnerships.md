# Clean-room measurement partnerships

## One-liner
Let advertisers measure outcomes against their own data without either side seeing the other's users.

## Why it matters
Large advertisers increasingly will not commit budget they cannot attribute, and the identifiers that used to carry attribution are gone. Clean rooms are how the rest of the industry solved this, and being absent from them means being excluded from the planning conversation entirely. It is a measurement problem in form and a revenue problem in substance.

## What ships
Integrations with the major clean-room providers, a privacy-reviewed data contract governing what leaves our side, aggregate-only outputs with enforced minimum thresholds, and a standard reach-and-frequency methodology so results are comparable across partners.

## Who it's for
Large advertisers and their measurement partners.

## Target outcome
Enough of our large advertisers can measure in their preferred clean room that it stops appearing as an objection in renewal conversations.

## Open questions
- Do we support every major provider or standardise on two? Every additional one is a distinct privacy review and a distinct maintenance burden.
- Does data residency (PLATFORM-007) force a per-region clean-room posture?
