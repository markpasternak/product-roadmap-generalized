# Attention-based pricing

## One-liner
Price inventory on measured attention rather than delivered impressions.

## Why it matters
Audio has a genuine attention advantage over most digital formats and no way to charge for it, because the currency is impressions and impressions treat a background tab the same as a commute. If attention becomes a tradeable currency we are structurally advantaged; if we build it and the market ignores it we have an expensive research project. That asymmetry is worth exploring, carefully.

## What ships
Undefined. Discovery needs a defensible attention metric that a third party would validate, which is the hard part.

## Who it's for
Advertisers and agencies buying on outcome rather than volume.

## Target outcome
A metric methodology credible enough for an independent measurement body to consider.

## Open questions
- Can attention be measured in audio without device signals we should not be collecting?
- Does anyone buy on a currency only one seller offers?
