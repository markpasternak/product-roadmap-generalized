# Retail media and commerce signals

## One-liner
Partner with retailers so purchase outcomes can close the loop on audio campaigns.

## Why it matters
Audio's persistent weakness is proving it sold anything. Retail media networks hold the purchase signal, and pairing our reach with their outcome data is the strongest attribution story available to us. Validation is the right stage because the commercial terms — who owns the relationship, who reports the number — matter more than the integration.

## What ships
Not yet committed. Validation is running with two retail partners to establish whether the matched-audience overlap is large enough to produce a statistically usable read.

## Who it's for
Consumer packaged goods and retail advertisers.

## Target outcome
A defensible yes or no on whether overlap and match rates support a real product, before any build.

## Open questions
- Does this belong in the clean-room framework rather than as a separate integration?
- Are the partners willing to let us report the outcome number, or only to see it themselves?
