# Video takeover inventory

## One-liner
Sell the video ad slots that the video podcast rollout creates, rather than serving audio ads against a screen.

## Why it matters
Video podcast hours are growing fast and are currently monetised with audio creative, which is the lowest-value way to use the most valuable inventory we have added in years. Video CPMs are multiples of audio, and buyers already have the creative — we are the only thing missing. The dependency on the video rollout makes the timing tight but the case is unusually clear.

## What ships
Video ad slots in the video podcast playback path, creative specs and validation for the standard video formats, exchange and direct-sold availability, and viewability measurement wired into the clean-room work rather than a separate stack.

## Who it's for
Brand advertisers already buying video elsewhere; creators whose filmed shows currently monetise as audio.

## Target outcome
Monetise the large majority of video podcast hours at video rates, with creator payouts reflecting the higher yield.

## Open questions
- What happens when a listener is in audio-only fallback mid-episode — does the buy degrade to audio, or does the impression not count?
- Do creators get to decline video ads on their show, and what does that do to the exchange?
