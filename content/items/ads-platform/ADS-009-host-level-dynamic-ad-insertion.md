# Host-level dynamic ad insertion

## One-liner
Insert ads at the hosting layer so third-party-hosted shows can carry our demand.

## Why it matters
A large share of podcast listening happens on shows we do not host, which means our demand cannot reach it. Host-level insertion is how the rest of the podcast ad market solved this. It is in Later because it requires partnerships with hosting providers who are, in most cases, also competitors.

## What ships
Undefined. Discovery is a commercial feasibility question before a technical one.

## Who it's for
Advertisers wanting reach beyond our owned hosting; shows hosted elsewhere.

## Target outcome
Establish whether any major host would actually partner on acceptable terms.

## Open questions
- Is there a version of this that works without a hosting partnership at all?
