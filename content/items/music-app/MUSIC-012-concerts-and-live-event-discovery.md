# Concerts and live event discovery

## One-liner
Surface nearby shows by artists a listener actually streams, with ticketing handled by partners.

## Why it matters
We know who someone listens to and roughly where they are, which makes us better at predicting concert intent than any ticketing platform. It is in intake rather than on the roadmap because the value depends almost entirely on partner inventory quality per market, and we have not tested that. Artists want it, which matters for the Spotify for Artists relationship.

## What ships
Nothing yet. This needs a market-by-market read on partner coverage before it is worth scoping.

## Who it's for
Listeners with strong artist affinity in markets with good live coverage.

## Target outcome
Decide whether coverage is good enough in enough markets to be worth building, or whether this is permanently a two-market feature.

## Open questions
- Is a partner integration enough, or does this only work if we own the inventory relationship?
- How do we handle the markets where coverage is poor without the feature reading as broken?
