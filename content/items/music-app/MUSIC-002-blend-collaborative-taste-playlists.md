# Blend: collaborative taste playlists

## One-liner
A shared playlist that merges two or more listeners’ taste profiles and keeps updating as they listen.

## Why it matters
Social features on a listening product usually fail because they ask people to perform taste in public. Blend asks for nothing — it reads what people already listen to and produces an artefact worth sharing. It was our cheapest test of whether personalisation could be a social object rather than a private one, and the answer was clearly yes.

## What ships
Invite-by-link Blends for up to ten participants, a per-track attribution strip showing whose taste pulled each song in, a weekly refresh, and a shareable taste-match card. Also the first production consumer of the shared taste-vector service.

## Who it's for
Listeners with at least one other account they share music with — friends, partners, families.

## Target outcome
Establish a repeatable social loop that drives return visits without a feed. Retention among Blend participants ran materially above matched controls.
