# Lyrics-led sing-along mode

## One-liner
A full-screen lyrics mode with vocal attenuation and optional pitch feedback.

## Why it matters
It demos beautifully and the engagement data from the prototype was genuinely good. It is parked rather than dead because the blocker is not product quality — it is that vocal attenuation on the master recording needs rights terms we do not currently have, and negotiating them is not worth the leverage we would spend right now.

## What ships
Nothing until the rights position changes. The prototype and its findings are kept so this can restart from where it stopped rather than from zero.

## Who it's for
Younger listeners and shared-device contexts, based on the prototype cohort.

## Target outcome
None while parked. Revisit when a broader rights conversation is already open for another reason.

## Open questions
- Is there a version using only the instrumental stems we already license, and is that version still good?
