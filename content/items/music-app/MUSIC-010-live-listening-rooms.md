# Live listening rooms

## One-liner
Synchronised group listening with a host, a queue anyone can add to, and a live reaction track.

## Why it matters
Blend proved shared taste is a compelling object; rooms test whether shared time is. Listening together is one of the few things a music service can do that a recommendation engine cannot, and it is the natural home for release-day moments, artist sessions and fan communities. It is also the hardest moderation surface we have contemplated, which is the honest reason it sits in Later.

## What ships
Undefined. Discovery needs to answer whether synchronisation at scale is affordable, whether hosting is a real behaviour or a novelty, and what moderation costs before we would commit.

## Who it's for
Fan communities and, if it works, artists running release-day sessions.

## Target outcome
A clear read on whether this is a product or a feature, with a moderation cost estimate attached to the answer.

## Open questions
- Who moderates a live room, and what happens at 3am in a language we do not staff?
- Do rights agreements cover synchronised group playback in every market we would launch in?
