# Audiobook clubs

## One-liner
Shared reading progress, spoiler-safe discussion and a shared pace for a group listening to the same title.

## Why it matters
Book clubs are a durable behaviour that predates every platform, and they are one of the few social structures that increases completion rather than distracting from it. If audiobook completion drives publisher settlement and renewal, a mechanic that raises it is worth more than its engagement numbers suggest. It depends on the in-app messaging work landing first.

## What ships
Undefined. Blocked on MUSIC-005 proving out an in-app conversation surface worth building on.

## Who it's for
Audiobook listeners who already coordinate outside the app.

## Target outcome
Establish whether shared pacing measurably lifts completion before committing to build it.

## Open questions
- Spoiler safety in a threaded discussion is genuinely hard. Is progress-gated visibility enough?
