# Audiobook top-ups and à la carte purchase

## One-liner
Buy more listening time, or a single title outright, when the monthly allowance runs out.

## Why it matters
The allowance created a class of listener who finishes it early and then simply stops — the moment of highest intent is the moment we hand them a wall. Top-ups convert that intent directly and give publishers a second settlement path for their most-consumed titles, which strengthens our position in the next licensing round. It is the clearest revenue follow-on from the bundle.

## What ships
Time top-ups and single-title purchase, in-context offers at the point the allowance is exhausted, purchased titles that persist across plan changes and cancellation, and publisher settlement covering both models.

## Who it's for
Heavy audiobook listeners who exhaust the allowance, roughly a fifth of active audiobook users.

## Target outcome
Recover most of the listening currently lost at the allowance wall, and add an incremental revenue line that does not reduce Premium conversion.

## Open questions
- Does an aggressive top-up prompt make the included allowance feel like a trial rather than a benefit?
- Store billing terms differ by platform. Does the offer surface differ too, or do we absorb the margin difference?
