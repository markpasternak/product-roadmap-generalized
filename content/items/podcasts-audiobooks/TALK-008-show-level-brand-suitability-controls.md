# Show-level brand suitability controls

## One-liner
Per-episode suitability classification that advertisers can target against and creators can see and contest.

## Why it matters
Advertisers will not commit meaningful budget to podcast inventory they cannot reason about, and today the only lever they have is a blunt show-level block. That costs creators money on episodes that were never a problem. Episode-level classification serves both sides at once, and the transcript work makes it newly feasible.

## What ships
An episode-level classifier over transcripts aligned to the standard industry suitability framework, advertiser targeting and exclusion by tier, a creator-facing view of how each episode was classified, and an appeal path with human review.

## Who it's for
Advertisers buying podcast inventory, and creators whose monetisation currently suffers from over-blocking.

## Target outcome
Increase the share of catalogue hours that are monetisable while reducing advertiser-reported suitability incidents.

## Open questions
- Who owns the appeal SLA — this team or trust operations? The answer changes the staffing ask materially.
- Do we expose the classification to listeners, or only to creators and advertisers?
