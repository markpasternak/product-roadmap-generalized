# Chapters, transcripts and in-episode search

## One-liner
Every episode gets machine-generated chapters and a synchronised, searchable transcript.

## Why it matters
Long-form audio has no equivalent of skimming, which makes a ninety-minute episode a bigger commitment than its content warrants. Transcripts make audio navigable, searchable and accessible in one move, and they turned out to be the highest-leverage accessibility work available to us. They also made episode content legible to ranking for the first time.

## What ships
Automatic transcription across the catalogue with word-level sync, chapter detection with creator override, in-episode search, and an accessibility-reviewed reading view. Transcripts are also exposed to the metadata graph for ranking and brand-suitability classification.

## Who it's for
Listeners who skim or search, deaf and hard-of-hearing users, and non-native speakers.

## Target outcome
Cover the substantial majority of active catalogue hours with usable transcripts and improve episode completion for long-form shows. Achieved.
