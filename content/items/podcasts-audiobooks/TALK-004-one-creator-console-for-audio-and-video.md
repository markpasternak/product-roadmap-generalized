# One creator console for audio and video

## One-liner
Collapse the three separate publishing tools creators use today into one console.

## Why it matters
A creator publishing a filmed episode currently touches three systems with three different notions of what a show is, and the seams produce most of our creator support volume. Consolidation is not a feature, it is the precondition for shipping anything else to creators without paying the integration tax three times. It also gives us one place to reason about monetisation eligibility.

## What ships
A single console with one show model, unified upload for audio and video, one analytics surface, migration for existing shows without a republish, and a stable API that the Spotify for Artists podcaster tools build on rather than duplicate.

## Who it's for
Every publishing creator, with the sharpest benefit for shows that publish both audio and video.

## Target outcome
Retire the two legacy consoles, and cut creator support contacts about publishing by a substantial margin.

## Open questions
- How long do we run the legacy consoles read-only? Some large shows have deep automation against the old APIs.
- Does the shared show model belong here or in the content metadata graph (PLATFORM-005)?
