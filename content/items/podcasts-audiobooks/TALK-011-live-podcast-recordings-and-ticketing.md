# Live podcast recordings and ticketing

## One-liner
Ticketed live recordings streamed to fans, with the episode published afterwards.

## Why it matters
Creators ask for this regularly and the fan demand is real. It is parked because it requires live infrastructure, a payments surface and an events operation we do not have, and standing all three up for one format is not defensible against anything else on this list. Revisit if the live infrastructure appears for another reason.

## What ships
Nothing.

## Who it's for
Large shows with an engaged live audience.

## Target outcome
None while parked.
