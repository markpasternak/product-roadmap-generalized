# Partner Program payouts v2

## One-liner
Rebuild creator payouts around consumption and engagement rather than an ad-impression proxy.

## Why it matters
The current model pays creators on a proxy that correlates badly with the value their show actually creates, which means our best creators are systematically underpaid relative to their contribution and know it. Every conversation about exclusivity, video investment or catalogue commitment eventually reaches this, so it gates a lot of strategy. It is also the item most likely to expose accounting problems we would rather find ourselves.

## What ships
A payout model driven by consumed minutes, engagement quality and format, monthly statements a creator can reconcile line by line, a projection tool so payout is predictable before the month closes, and a dispute path with a defined resolution window.

## Who it's for
Every creator in the Partner Program, with the largest shifts among high-completion long-form shows.

## Target outcome
A payout a creator can predict and verify, with dispute volume falling rather than rising after the transition.

## Open questions
- Who absorbs the cost of creators whose payout falls under the new model — do we grandfather, taper, or take the conversation?
- Does engagement quality open a fraud surface we are not currently defending?
