# Audiobooks included in Premium

## One-liner
A monthly allowance of audiobook listening bundled into the existing Premium subscription.

## Why it matters
Audiobooks were a purchase, and purchases are a different product with a different funnel — one we were not good at. Bundling an allowance turned the question from "will you buy this book" into "have you tried this yet", which is a question our whole surface is built to answer. It also gave publishers a reason to license into a subscription model they had resisted for a decade.

## What ships
A metered monthly allowance with clear remaining-time surfacing, publisher settlement based on consumed minutes rather than checkouts, an entitlement model that survives plan changes and household sharing, and audiobook rows in existing discovery surfaces.

## Who it's for
Existing Premium subscribers, especially the large group who had never opened an audiobook.

## Target outcome
Convert a meaningful share of Premium subscribers into audiobook listeners in the first two quarters, and hold publisher settlement within the negotiated envelope. Both landed.
