# Video podcasts on every surface

## One-liner
Video episodes that play natively on mobile, desktop, TV and in the car, and degrade cleanly to audio.

## Why it matters
The largest podcasts are now filmed by default and are distributed primarily where video plays well. If we are audio-only for those shows we are a secondary destination for our own biggest creators, and the audience follows the creator. The hard part is not video playback — it is that half of podcast listening happens in contexts where a screen is unusable, so video cannot become the requirement.

## What ships
A video path through the existing playback pipeline, automatic audio-only fallback driven by context rather than by a setting, picture-in-picture and background audio continuity, TV and car surfaces, and creator upload with per-episode video controls.

## Who it's for
Video-first creators and the audiences following them; existing audio listeners must see no regression.

## Target outcome
Video-native distribution for the top filmed shows without reducing audio-only listening hours for the same shows.

## Open questions
- Does a video episode count toward audiobook-style consumption accounting, or does it need its own settlement model?
- How aggressively do we auto-fallback? Too eager and video creators feel unsupported; too reluctant and we burn mobile data.
