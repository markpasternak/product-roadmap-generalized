# Structured courses and learning catalogue

## One-liner
Sequenced, progress-tracked educational audio alongside podcasts and audiobooks.

## Why it matters
A meaningful share of spoken-word listening is already educational in intent but served by content with no structure, no progress model and no completion signal. Courses are the natural third spoken-word format and the one with the clearest willingness to pay. It is in intake because it needs a catalogue strategy we do not have — this is a licensing and commissioning question long before it is a product one.

## What ships
Nothing yet. Needs a catalogue read first.

## Who it's for
Listeners with existing educational listening behaviour.

## Target outcome
Decide whether there is a licensable catalogue worth building a format for.

## Open questions
- Is this a licensing play, a commissioning play, or a creator-tools play? All three have been proposed and they are very different products.
