# Branching and interactive audio formats

## One-liner
Episodes where a listener choice changes what plays next.

## Why it matters
Audio is the last major medium with no interactive form, which is either a genuine gap or strong evidence that listeners do not want one. Discovery exists to tell those apart cheaply. The honest prior is that it is a novelty, and the reason to look anyway is that the production tooling would be reusable for adaptive advertising and education formats even if the entertainment case fails.

## What ships
Nothing committed. A small prototype with two or three commissioned pieces, tested against a straight linear cut of the same material.

## Who it's for
Unknown. The prototype exists to find out whether there is an audience at all.

## Target outcome
A clear kill-or-continue answer within one quarter of prototype work.

## Open questions
- Is the interesting output the format, or the production tooling underneath it?
